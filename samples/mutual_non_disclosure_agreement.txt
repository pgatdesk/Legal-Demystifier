MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AND CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between TechInnovate Corp ("Company") and Partner Solutions LLC ("Recipient").

1. PURPOSE
The parties wish to explore a potential business collaboration regarding artificial intelligence workflows and enterprise legal automation ("Purpose").

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary information, technical data, trade secrets, software architecture, financial plans, customer lists, and business operations disclosed directly or indirectly. Any idea or verbal statement made during meetings shall automatically be treated as Confidential Information without requiring written designation.

3. OBLIGATIONS & RESTRICTIONS
Each party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strictest confidence and not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent. The recipient agrees not to reverse engineer, decompile, or copy any sample material provided.

4. DURATION & PERPETUAL RESTRICTION
The obligations of non-disclosure and non-use under this Agreement shall survive the termination of discussions and shall remain in effect in perpetuity (forever) with respect to all disclosed information, regardless of whether it becomes generally known in the industry.

5. NON-SOLICITATION & NON-COMPETITION
During the term of this Agreement and for a period of three (3) years thereafter, neither party shall directly or indirectly solicit, hire, or engage any employee or contractor of the other party. Recipient further covenants that it shall not develop, commercialize, or invest in any competing software product within the legal AI domain worldwide for 24 months.

6. REMEDIES & INJUNCTIVE RELIEF
Any unauthorized disclosure shall cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate. Disclosing party is entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief and liquidated damages of $250,000 per violation without the necessity of posting bond or proving actual financial loss.

7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and any dispute shall be resolved exclusively in the Court of Chancery of Delaware.
