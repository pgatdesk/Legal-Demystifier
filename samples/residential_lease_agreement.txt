RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on this 1st day of October, between Apex Realty Management ("Landlord") and John Doe ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES & TERM
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the residential premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Unit 4B, for a term of 12 months commencing November 1.

2. RENT & AUTOMATIC ESCALATION
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent of $1,850 payable in advance on the first day of each month. Landlord reserves the absolute right to increase the monthly rent by up to 15% at any time during the tenancy with 7 days written notice. Late payments occurring after the 2nd day of the month shall incur a $150 penalty plus $25 per day.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT & FORFEITURE
Tenant shall deposit $3,700 as security. Tenant explicitly agrees that if Tenant terminates this agreement prior to the full 12-month term for any reason, the entire security deposit shall be deemed non-refundable liquidated damages. Landlord shall have 90 business days post-move-out to account for any deductions.

4. UNRESTRICTED ACCESS
Landlord and its contractors reserve the right to enter the leased premises at any time, 24 hours a day, without prior notice, for inspection, repairs, appraisals, or showing the property to prospective buyers or tenants.

5. MAINTENANCE & INDEMNIFICATION
Tenant assumes full responsibility for all maintenance, repairs, plumbing leaks, HVAC upkeep, and structural defects regardless of cause. Tenant agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Landlord against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, or injuries occurring on the property, even if caused by Landlord's negligence or property decay.

6. QUIET ENJOYMENT & GUESTS
No overnight guests are permitted for more than two consecutive nights without written consent and an additional fee of $50 per guest per night. Any noise audible outside the unit after 9:00 PM shall constitute a material breach subject to immediate eviction.

7. TERMINATION & ATTORNEYS' FEES
If Landlord initiates any legal proceeding, Tenant shall be responsible for all of Landlord's legal fees and costs, regardless of whether Landlord prevails in court. Tenant waives all rights to a jury trial.
