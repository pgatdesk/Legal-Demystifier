5. NON-COMPETE & EXCLUSIVITY
During the project and for twelve (12) months following project completion, Contractor shall not perform design or development services for any company in the digital media, SaaS, or advertising sector.

Client may terminate this Agreement immediately at any time for convenience without cause. In the event of early termination, Client shall have no obligation to pay Contractor for any work completed up to the date of termination.
